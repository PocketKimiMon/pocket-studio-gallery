About

hi, i'm mykey.

[ Mykey photo ]
replace with your portrait

"I started doing hair because I saw too many people leave salons feeling smaller than when they walked in. The small talk, the mirrors, the rushed energy — it doesn't work for everyone. It didn't always work for me."

I'm a hair artist, barber, and systems nerd. I build calm spaces where people feel safe enough to look like themselves. The chair should be the safest seat in your week.

So I built a practice that puts comfort first. Extra time for people who need it. Clear communication. No pressure to perform. And services like the AuDHD First-Time Haircut and Silent Cut, because I think hairdressing should include the people who usually get left out.

I work in Seattle and travel up to 30 miles for house calls. When I'm not behind the chair, I'm building automations and finding ways to make the admin disappear, so the creative work can stay human.
