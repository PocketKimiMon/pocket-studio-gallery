the fine print

so nobody gets surprised
  • 01

    one month at a time

    the calendar opens one month at a time, first come first serve. on the 1st of each month, the next full month becomes available.

  • 02

    advance notice

    haircuts book 2 days out. new-client color consults book 3 days out, and existing-client color appointments book 1 week out — color takes prep. need it sooner? .

  • 03

    24-hour cancellation

    need to cancel or reschedule? 24 hours notice, unless it's an actual emergency. just talk to me — i'm reasonable, but my time is how i pay rent.

  • 04

    no-call-no-show = charged

    miss a confirmed appointment without timely notice and you may be charged up to the full quoted/estimated service amount (invoiced if no card on file). fairness goes both ways — full terms on the terms page.

  • 05

    2-hour verification

    you'll get a text or email 2 hours before your appointment to confirm. if i don't hear back, i may release the slot.

  • 06

    pricing & payment

    prices are quoted before or at service (they can vary by hair/complexity). payment is due at the appointment unless we agree otherwise.

  • 07

    house-call space

    provide a safe, ready spot and an accurate address. i may decline or end a visit if the location isn't workable. disclose allergies & prior chemical work.
