the founder brand

mykey pocket

Beauty-chair intimacy meets a systems brain. One person quietly running a studio in their pocket — building the automations and little digital tools that keep salons full, wrapped in brat-era chaos and made for brains that don't do "normal."

01

the feeling

how it should hit before a single word is read

It should feel like your sharpest friend pulled up a chair, handed you a coffee, and said “okay, here's actually what's wrong with your booking system.”

Warm enough to trust with your business. Weird enough to remember. Under the glitter there's a spreadsheet that works — the chaos is the personality, the precision is the product.

Half grimoire, half dashboard. Loud color, real craft, zero corporate cosplay. It never talks down, never pads, and never pretends to be a five-person agency. It's one brain, out loud.

electric, intimate, a little feral —
but the receipts always add up.

06

voice & style

how it talks

do

  • lowercase by default — confidence doesn't shout in caps
  • talk like a person: “here's the fix,” not “leverage our solution”
  • be blunt, then be kind — name the problem, then hold their hand
  • let the receipts talk (40% fewer no-shows > “transformative results”)
  • one joke, one wink, then back to the point
  • own being one brain: “I built this” beats “our team”

don't

  • corporate cosplay — no “synergy,” “robust,” “seamless,” “solutions”
  • fake a big team or a big office you don't have
  • bury the point under three paragraphs of throat-clearing
  • talk down to people who aren't technical
  • drown the design in acid lime — it's a spice, not the meal
  • over-apologize or over-hedge — say the thing
sounds like

“your calendar isn't full because you're bad at this. it's full of holes nobody's plugging. i plug holes.”

“a whole studio that fits in your pocket — booking, reminders, rebooking, done while you're mid-blowout.”

“built by a cosmetologist who got tired of watching good chairs sit empty. weird on purpose. works on purpose.”
